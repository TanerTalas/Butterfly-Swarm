The butterfly mark

One drawing, used everywhere in the interface. It is not the 3D butterfly that flies in the meadow — that one is built from geometry and a texture atlas. This is the flat mark: release preview, profile avatar, colour swatches, list rows.

Geometry is identical to the shipping component. Two wing paths are drawn for the right side only; the left side is a mirror, so symmetry is exact by construction rather than by hand.

The mark

Forewing and hindwing are separate paths so each can take its own colour.

Simple drops the antennae and nothing else. Below roughly 40 px the antennae collapse into two grey specks that read as dirt, so every small placement uses this variant.

Rules

Only the right side is drawn. The left is scale(-1,1) translate(-124,0) of the same path, so symmetry cannot drift.
Forewing and hindwing stay separate paths. Members colour them independently; guests get one colour on both. Merging them removes a product feature.
Body colour is fixed. #43332F. It is the only hard-coded colour in the drawing — everything else is passed in.
It must survive 24 px. The smallest placement is a colour swatch. Anything that turns to mush at that size does not belong in the mark.
The aspect ratio is 124 : 100. Every size in the table above keeps it. Stretching the mark is not a variant.
